# Innovate@DIAB Website

The official website for **Innovate@DIAB**, a student-led creative project.

## About

This site is the online home of our project. It previews the creative work we'll be producing over the coming weeks and gives anyone, whether they go to our school or not, a way to get involved.

## Features

- **Sign-up page:** lets students at our school and people outside it register to take part.
- **Project preview:** shows the creative work we're planning and what we hope to achieve.
- **Meet the Team:** introduces the people behind the project.
- **Social media links:** links to all our accounts so visitors can follow along.
- **Vintage-inspired design:** includes our logo and old-time design elements.
