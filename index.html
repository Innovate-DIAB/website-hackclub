About Us

Students who build, not just learn.

Innovate @ DIABis the Hack Club at Dubai International Academy Al Barsha. Instead of just learning about technology, we use it to make real things: apps, games, websites, AI tools, 3D designs and anything else we can dream up.

We work on projects for Hack Club programs, where students who finish and ship their projects can earn real prizes. You don't need any experience. You just need to be curious.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Who can join DIAB Innovate?

Anyone! Every age and every grade is welcome, and so are complete beginners.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. We'll help you learn as you build. Lots of people start with zero experience.

What is Hack Club?

Hack Club is a global nonprofit network of high school coding clubs. It runs programs where students build projects and can earn prizes for shipping them.

When is the first meeting?

DIAB students: lunchtime on Wednesday, 25 November 2026. External members: online on Friday, 27 November 2026 at 4:00 PM GST (GMT+4). Check the bulletin board above for more meetings.

I'm not at DIAB. Can I still join?

Yes! Students from any school can join as external members. External members meet online. Use the External Sign Up button below.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining Innovate @ DIABis completely free.

What do I need to bring?

A laptop if you have one, and your ideas. That's it!

How do I join?

DIAB students use the DIAB sign-up form. Students from other schools use the External Sign Up form. Both buttons are just below. You can also email us.

Pick the sign-up that fits you. We'd love to have you.
